      FAQ

      Your question isn’t here? Write to us, we’re happy to help.

      Go to support
      How much does Dosano cost?

      Dosano is offered as a subscription on the App Store. You’ll see the current price in the App Store before you buy. You can cancel at any time in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name, then “Subscriptions”.

      Do I need an account?

      No. Dosano works without sign-up, without an email address and without an account.

      Where is my data stored?

      Only on your iPhone. Widgets and the Live Activity read the same data, also on the device. We don’t run a server and never see any of it.

      If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or your computer, the data is part of that backup.

      What happens if I miss a dose?

      If you like, Dosano reminds you up to three more times. If you took it and just didn’t check it off, you can log it afterwards with the right time. Whether you should make up a missed dose is something to discuss with your doctor or pharmacist.

      Which devices are supported?

      Dosano runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. There’s no Apple Watch version and no Apple Health integration yet.

      Does Dosano replace medical advice?

      No. Dosano is not a medical device but a reminder and note-taking aid. Please discuss which medications to take, when and how much with your doctor or pharmacist.
