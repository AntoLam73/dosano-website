Three things Dosano does really well.

Remind, log, restock. No clutter, no guilt, just a calm view of your day.

  • Reminds you right on timeOne notification for everything due together, repeated if you haven’t checked it off, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
  • Logs every doseTaken, late or skipped, with the time. The history calendar shows your month at a glance.
  • Watches your supplyKnows how long each pack lasts and tells you before it runs out. Scan a pack to restock in a tap.
