2. The Dosano app

Your data stays on your iPhone

Dosano works without an account and without a server of its own. Everything you enter in the app, or that is created while you use it, is stored only locally on your iPhone, in particular:

  • medications with name, strength, form, dosing times and notes,
  • the log of your doses (taken, late, skipped) with times,
  • supply, restocks and package sizes,
  • notes and photos you add to a medication,
  • your settings.

This information may be health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. We have no access to it: the app does not transmit anything to us or to third parties. Widgets, the Live Activity and Siri shortcuts read the same data through a shared storage area on your device (App Group). Here, too, the data never leaves your iPhone.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no advertising IDs.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. No server is needed for this.
  • Camera: when you scan a package, the app reads text and barcode with Apple’s VisionKit directly on the device. The camera is also used when you take a photo for a medication. Images are downscaled and stored only in the app.
  • Photos: if you pick an image from your library, the app only receives that one image.
  • Motion sensor: so that the pills in the pill jar roll when you tilt your iPhone, the app reads the device’s orientation. These values are neither stored nor shared.

You can change all permissions at any time in your iPhone’s Settings under “Dosano”.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Dosano with Siri, Apple processes your voice request according to Apple’s privacy policy. We receive nothing from this. Medications you have marked as “Discreet” are not named by Siri.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer, Dosano’s data is part of that backup. Whether and where backups are made is up to you in your iPhone’s settings. Apple is responsible for iCloud backups.

App Store, purchase and subscription

Dosano is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles downloads, purchases, subscriptions and payment under its own responsibility, and Apple’s privacy policy applies. We do not receive any names, email addresses or payment details from Apple.

Apple provides developers with aggregated statistics (for example the number of downloads and sales). Only if you have agreed in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers may Apple also send us crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. These do not allow any conclusions about you or your medications. You can withdraw this consent at any time under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Reporting bugs and suggesting ideas

“Report a bug” and “Suggest an idea” in the app’s settings open a prepared email in your mail app. It contains the app version, iOS version and iPhone model so that we can reproduce problems. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send” yourself, and you can edit the text beforehand. How we handle emails is described under Contact by email.

Deleting data

In the app, you can delete all entries under Settings, “Delete all data”. If you delete the app, all of its data is also removed from your iPhone. Data in older backups remains until those backups are replaced or deleted.

3. This website

Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data that your browser sends automatically. This includes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the previously visited page (referrer) and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, visitors’ IP addresses are stored for security purposes.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide the website reliably, securely and without a server of our own. You can find more information in GitHub’s privacy statement.

GitHub also processes data in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which GitHub is certified.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and uses no analytics or tracking services. Fonts, images and scripts are loaded directly from this website, not from third parties such as Google Fonts or content delivery networks. Consent under Section 25 of the German TDDDG is therefore not required.

Links to the App Store

If you click “Download on the App Store”, you are taken to Apple’s App Store. From then on, Apple’s privacy policy applies.

4. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address, your name (if given) and the content of your message in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns the use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering inquiries).

Our mailbox is hosted by Gmail, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Your message is therefore stored on Google’s servers, including in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which Google is certified. More in Google’s privacy policy.

Please don’t send us any health data we don’t need to answer you. We delete emails once your request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply, at the latest after two years.

Reminder at the app’s launch

With “Remind me at launch” you send us a prepared email. We then use your email address for one purpose only: to let you know once as soon as Dosano is available on the App Store. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending the email. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing dosano.help@gmail.com. We delete your address after the notification, at the latest after twelve months. We don’t pass it on and won’t send you any advertising.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to

  • access the data we process about you (Art. 15),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16),
  • erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),
  • withdraw your consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Just write to us at dosano.help@gmail.com. Since the app sends no data to us, we cannot provide information about your entries in the app: they exist only on your iPhone.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority where you live or the authority responsible for us: the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (datenschutz-berlin.de).

There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

6. Changes

If the app or this website changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.
