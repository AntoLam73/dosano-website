How can we help?

Something isn’t working, or you have an idea? Write to us, we read every message.

Write an email dosano.help@gmail.com

Tip: In the app’s settings you’ll find “Report a bug” and “Suggest an idea”. They put the app version, iOS version and iPhone model into the email for you, so we can help faster.

We answer as quickly as we can, usually within a few days. Please only send us the information we need to answer, and no photos of prescriptions or medical reports.

Common questions

Reminders aren’t showing up.

Open your iPhone’s Settings and tap Notifications, then Dosano. “Allow Notifications” must be turned on.

If a Focus is active, such as “Do Not Disturb” or “Sleep”, notifications may be silenced. You can add Dosano as an allowed app in that Focus.

The Live Activity doesn’t appear.

Check in your iPhone’s Settings under Dosano that “Live Activities” is turned on, and in the app’s settings that the Live Activity is enabled. According to Apple, a Live Activity runs for at most 8 hours. Opening the app renews it.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap “Edit”, then “Add Widget”. Search for Dosano and choose a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap “Customize” and then the widget area.

I’m getting a new iPhone.

Your data lives only on your iPhone, not with us. Set up your new iPhone from a backup of your old one (via iCloud or your computer), and your medications and history will be back.

How do I delete all my data?

In the app under Settings, “Delete all data”. If you delete the app, its data is removed from your iPhone as well. We don’t hold anything that would need deleting on our side.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name at the top and then “Subscriptions”. For refunds, please contact Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves.

The scan doesn’t recognize my package correctly.

Hold the package straight and in good light, with the side showing the name and strength. Always check the recognized details before saving and correct them if needed. Feel free to tell us which package doesn’t work (name and strength are enough), so we can improve recognition.

Questions about your medication

For questions about dosage, interactions or a missed dose, please ask your doctor or pharmacist. Dosano is not a medical device and cannot replace their advice.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe).
